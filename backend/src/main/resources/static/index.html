National Criminal Justice Informatics Architecture (§16 Interoperability)
Zero-Trust Adapter Fabric

CrimeNet interfaces seamlessly with India's five justice pillars under the Inter-operable Criminal Justice System (ICJS) framework administered by NCRB and NIC. Adapters enforce bilateral cryptographic provenance, automated synchronization, and strict protocol segregation.

ICJS Central Gateway Active Gateway

Integrates with e-Courts (hearings, bail status, charge sheet acceptance) and e-Prosecution.

Binds local police station General Diary (GD), First Information Reports (FIR), and IO assignments.

How Live Government Adapters Operate in Production

In a physical state/central deployment, ICJS & CCTNS never expose open public internet endpoints. Traffic is routed strictly through National Informatics Centre (NIC) leased-line infrastructure (NICNET / SWAN) via mutual TLS (mTLS) with departmental X.509 certificates and IP whitelisting. In CrimeNet, the Spring ExternalSystemAdapter abstraction allows seamless toggling between high-fidelity local simulation (development/staging) and production mTLS government endpoints via application.yml without modifying a single line of business logic.
