How Court Blockchain Verification Works (Without Exposing Confidential Evidence)

Under Indian criminal law (Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam 2023 §65B), crime scene photos, victim identities, and classified FIRs must never be placed on a public blockchain in readable form. CrimeNet solves this by only anchoring a mathematical master fingerprint (the Merkle Root) to the public Polygon ledger.

1. Off-Chain Privacy
Original PDFs and case files remain encrypted inside police servers. No public eye can view them.
2. Public Immutability
Only the master seal is anchored to Polygon blockchain. It can never be deleted or altered by anyone.
3. Instant Court Proof
Any judge or defense counsel can verify in 1 second that the evidence presented is 100% genuine.
